Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

This policy applies to Deep Bubble 1024 (深海啵啵 1024), Android package tech.zhongbei.deepbubble1024. It is published by Zhenwei Ruan (“we”, “us”).

In short

  • The current release works offline and does not create accounts.
  • Game progress and settings are stored on your device.
  • The current release does not show ads from an advertising network and does not include an advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDK.
  • We do not collect or receive personal data through gameplay in the current release.

Information and local storage

The game stores gameplay progress, scores, and settings in local app storage on your device so that you can continue playing. This information is not sent to us by the game. You can remove it by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. Device backup behavior, if enabled, is controlled by your Android settings.

The current release does not use an account, sign-in, cloud save, in-app purchase, or third-party advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting service. Some reward-ad controls are placeholders; they do not load or display an online ad in this release.

Children and families

Deep Bubble 1024 is intended for families, including children under 13. The current release does not ask children to create an account or provide personal information, and it does not transmit gameplay data from the device. Parents or guardians can contact us at hello@zhongbei.tech with privacy questions.

Future rewarded ads

We may add optional rewarded ads in a future release. The current release has no live ad network or ad SDK. Before any future release adds ads, we will update this policy and the Google Play disclosures to describe the advertising service and the data it processes. Ads shown to children will be configured to meet the applicable Google Play Families requirements.

Support messages, security, and retention

If you email us, we receive the email address and the information you choose to include so we can respond. We keep support correspondence only as long as needed to handle the request and meet applicable obligations. We do not receive game save data through support unless you choose to include it in your message.

Gameplay data remains in the app's local storage. We do not maintain a gameplay-data account or server-side game save for this release. The policy page is hosted by Cloudflare, which may process basic connection information to deliver and protect the page; see Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.

Changes and contact

If the app's data practices change, we will update this page before releasing those changes. For privacy questions, contact the publisher:

Zhenwei Ruan
Email: hello@zhongbei.tech

隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 11 月 25 日

本政策适用于《深海啵啵 1024》（Deep Bubble 1024），Android 包名 tech.zhongbei.deepbubble1024。发布者为 Zhenwei Ruan（以下简称“我们”）。

简要说明

  • 当前版本可离线游玩，不创建用户账号。
  • 游戏进度、分数和设置保存在你的设备上。
  • 当前版本不展示广告网络提供的广告，也不包含广告、分析或崩溃报告 SDK。
  • 当前版本的游戏过程不会向我们收集或传输个人数据。

信息与本地存储

游戏将进度、分数和设置保存在设备的应用存储中，供你继续游玩；游戏不会把这些数据发送给我们。你可以通过清除应用存储或卸载应用删除这些数据。如启用了设备备份，其行为由你的 Android 设置控制。

当前版本不使用账号、登录、云存档、应用内购买或第三方广告、分析、崩溃报告服务。部分奖励广告入口目前只是占位功能，不会加载或展示在线广告。

儿童与家庭

《深海啵啵 1024》面向家庭用户，包括 13 岁以下儿童。当前版本不会要求儿童创建账号或提供个人信息，也不会将设备上的游戏数据传出。家长或监护人如有隐私问题，可通过 hello@zhongbei.tech 联系我们。

未来的激励广告

未来版本可能加入由玩家自主选择观看的激励广告。当前版本没有接入广告网络或广告 SDK。在任何未来版本加入广告前，我们会更新本政策和 Google Play 披露，说明广告服务及其处理的数据；面向儿童展示的广告将按适用的 Google Play Families 要求配置。

客服邮件、安全与保留期限

如果你给我们发送邮件，我们会收到你的邮箱地址和你在邮件中主动提供的信息，以便回复。我们仅在处理请求和履行适用义务所需期间保留客服邮件。除非你主动在邮件中提供，我们不会通过客服渠道收到游戏存档。

当前版本的游戏数据保存在应用本地；我们不维护游戏数据账号或服务端存档。本隐私政策页面由 Cloudflare 托管，Cloudflare 可能为传输和保护页面而处理基本连接信息，详见 Cloudflare 隐私政策。
